Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

codeKia operates an automated comment-response system for educational videos and Reels published on Instagram. This policy explains how information is processed when users comment on our content.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the information provided through the Instagram API, we may process comment text, comment identifiers, usernames, account identifiers, media identifiers, timestamps, and limited conversation context needed to manage responses.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information to identify generic or repetitive questions and reactions, generate relevant replies, prevent duplicate processing, and identify comments that require manual review.

4. Artificial Intelligence

Our system may use an AI service to classify comments and generate responses. AI-generated responses may be inaccurate. Comments that cannot be handled confidently may be excluded from automatic replies or referred for manual review.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be processed by Meta/Instagram, AI service providers, and infrastructure providers when necessary to operate the application. We do not sell personal information or share it for third-party advertising purposes.

6. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in our application database and technical logs to support processing, conversation context, duplicate prevention, and troubleshooting. We retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information processed by our application, contact us at codekia.online@gmail.com. Include relevant comment details or your Instagram username. Do not send passwords or access tokens. We will review requests and take appropriate action under applicable law.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Third-Party Services

Our application relies on services such as Instagram/Meta, AI providers, and infrastructure providers. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply to their processing.

10. Children's Privacy

Our content may be accessible to a broad audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, please contact us using the email above.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this policy when our application or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
